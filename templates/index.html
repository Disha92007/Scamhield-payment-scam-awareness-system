The 4 Golden Rules of Payment Safety

Pause, verify, and protect your financial information.

▣CRUCIAL RULE #1

UPI PIN is ONLY for Debiting Money

You never need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

A UPI PIN authorizes a payment from your account. Never enter it to receive cashback, a refund, or a buyer's payment.

▦CRUCIAL RULE #2

QR Codes Only SEND Money

Scanning a QR code can lead to a payment request.

Be suspicious if someone says to scan a code and enter your PIN to receive money for an item you are selling.

▣CRUCIAL RULE #3

Beware Fake Payment Screenshots

Never trust a screenshot as proof of credit.

Fraudsters can edit screenshots. Check the transaction in your own bank app before handing over goods.

◷CRUCIAL RULE #4

Bank Officials Never Ask for OTP

Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs.

Do not share these details over calls, SMS, or chat. Contact your bank using an official channel.
